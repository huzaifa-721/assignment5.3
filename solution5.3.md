I created a typography showcase webpage using two complementary Google Fonts: Playfair Display for headings and Poppins for body text. I linked them via Google Fonts CDN and set different fonts for heading and body to create visual hierarchy. I used different font sizes (48px, 36px, 28px, 16px) and weights (300 light, 400 regular, 600 semibold, 700 bold) to show variation. I applied text-align with left, center, justify and right alignments, text-decoration with underline and line-through, and font-style italic in different sections. I adjusted letter-spacing from 1px to 5px and line-height from 1.6 to 2 for improved readability and stylish effects. I divided the page into three required sections: Heading Styles showing different heading levels, Paragraph Styles showing alignment and spacing differences, and Text Emphasis demonstrating bold, italic, underline, strikethrough and spaced text effects.
